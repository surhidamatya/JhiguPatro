## Result (BS 1970–2100)

- This repo's table is identical to nepal_kit's for every year it covers.
- 17 years have at least one disagreement: **2062** and **2084–2099**.
- **2062:** only nepal-date-kit differs (months 1–2 are `31,31` vs `30,32` elsewhere). Three other sources agree with this repo.
- **2084–2099:** nepal-date-kit and nepali-date-converter agree with each other (through 2090) and disagree with this repo / nepal_kit / @remotemerge. In several years the two camps differ by a full day-count (for example 2084: 366 vs 365 days).
- **2087:** three-way split. nepal-date-kit sums to 367 days, which is implausible for a BS year.
- **2093:** @remotemerge also splits from this repo (months 9–10 `30,29` vs `29,30`).
- All five sources agree on BS 2000–2061 and 2063–2083.
